# Cartoon / cute RPG CC0 pack

These are selected specifically for a playful/cartoon RPG direction. All listed audio sources are marked CC0 on OpenGameArt.

## Priority packs

1. 80 CC0 creature SFX — 80 effects, including 10 cute, burble, burp, cough, eat, grunt, hurt, monster, roar, slime/weird-style creature sounds.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx
   ZIP: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/80-CC0-creature-SFX.zip

2. Zombies Sound Pack — 24 zombie sounds in WAV, created for the FOSS RPG Summoning Wars.
   Source: https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack
   ZIP: https://lpc.opengameart.org/sites/default/files/zombies.zip

3. Zombie / Skeleton / Monster Voice Effects — WAV voice effects, CC0.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-skeleton-monster-voice-effects
   ZIP: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Voice%20Effects%20Zombie-Skeleton-Monster%20Human%20Male.zip

4. 80 CC0 RPG SFX — blades, creatures, items, coins, gems, spells, fire, magic, wood, stone.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx
   ZIP: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/80-CC0-RPG-SFX.zip

5. 27 Metal Audio Samples — explicitly described by the author as suitable for cartoon/cartoon-related effects; CC0.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/27-metal-audio-samples-sfx
   ZIP: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/27-Metal-Audio-Samples-blacklodgegames.com_.zip

6. Dynamite sound effect — cartoon-tagged explosion/detonation; CC0.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/dynamite-sound-effect

7. Little Grunts — cartoon-tagged vocal/grunt effect; CC0.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/little-grunts

8. Accordion Sound — cartoon/comic sound; CC0.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/accordion-sound

9. Propeller (Cartoon) Loop — cartoon-tagged SFX; CC0.
   Source: https://opengameart.org/content/propeller-cartoon-loop

## Style target

The first four packs cover the actual RPG/zombie content. The latter packs are useful for making the presentation less horror-realistic and more goofy/cartoon-like.
