## MP3 conversion rule

The final archive is intended to contain **MP3 only**. Where a CC0 source pack is WAV/OGG/FLAC, the build process converts each source sound to MP3; the source license is retained in the manifest. No CC-BY/CC-BY-SA assets are included.
